SMS Privacy Policy

Parker Lazeski Alerts · Parker Lazeski (sole proprietor) · Last updated September 28, 2026

What this covers

Parker Lazeski Alerts sends automated text messages (website sign-up and sale alerts, and appointment and schedule reminders) to people who sign up on the sign-up form. This policy explains how their mobile numbers are handled.

Information collected

We keep each subscriber's name, mobile number, and a record of their consent (the consent wording, date and time, and IP address of the sign-up). It is used only to send the messages described above and to show that consent was given.

Sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Messages are delivered through Twilio, which processes the number only to deliver the message.

Opting out

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts. Reply HELP for help. Message and data rates may apply.
