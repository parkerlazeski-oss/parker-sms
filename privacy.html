SMS Privacy Policy

Parker Lazeski (sole proprietor) · Last updated September 28, 2026

What this covers

Parker Lazeski sends personal text messages (updates, reminders and website alerts) to a small number of family members who have agreed to receive them. This policy explains how the mobile numbers of those recipients are handled.

Information collected

The only information kept is each recipient's name, mobile number and their consent to receive texts. It is used only to send those texts.

Sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Messages are delivered through Twilio, which processes the number only to deliver the message.

Opting out

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts. Reply HELP for help. Message and data rates may apply.
