Program

Parker Lazeski Alerts sends automated text messages to people who sign up: alerts when a customer signs up or pays on a website Parker operates, and appointment and schedule reminders from Parker. No marketing or promotional messages are sent.

How you join

Text messages are optional. On the sign-up form you can sign up for email alerts only; you get texts only if you enter your mobile number and tick the separate, optional text box. You then get one confirmation text. Consent to texts is not a condition of signing up or of any purchase.

Frequency and cost

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

Stopping and help

Reply STOP at any time to cancel; you will get one confirmation and no further messages. Reply HELP for help, or email parkerlazeski@gmail.com.

Carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
